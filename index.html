02 · Student Physics & Why It Stays

Why every kilogram has a price.

Why didn’t astronauts fly their entire spacecraft home? Because escaping gravity obeys an exponential law—and every extra kilogram on the return trip multiplies the fuel required at launch.

PRINCIPLE 01 · TSIOLKOVSKY ROCKET EQUATION

Fuel scales exponentially

A rocket must carry the fuel needed to lift its remaining fuel. Dropping empty tanks and unneeded equipment slashes the mass that must be accelerated back into orbit.

PRINCIPLE 02 · TWO-STAGE LANDER DESIGN

The base becomes a launch pad

Apollo’s golden Descent Stage carried landing legs, radar, and braking propellant. At liftoff, explosive bolts severed the stages so the lighter Ascent Stage could blast off.

PRINCIPLE 03 · ONE-WAY ROBOTIC EXPLORERS

Built to rove, not return

Robotic landers and rovers carry no ascent rockets. Once lunar night or Martian dust ends their power, their final coordinates become protected heritage sites.

Student Analogy

Think of climbing a steep mountain: To bring home rare mineral samples from the summit, you leave your heavy base-camp tent and empty food canisters behind so you only carry the samples down. On the Moon, leaving behind the 2,034 kg descent stage saved thousands of kilograms of rocket propellant.
